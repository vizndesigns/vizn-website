Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This page explains what information VIZN ("we," "us") collects when you use this website or place an order, and how it's used. We keep this simple: we don't sell your data, and we only use it to design and deliver your order.

Information We Collect

When you submit an order through our order form, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Name, email, and phone number
  • Team, school, or organization name
  • Design details (sport, colors, photos, and other brief content you provide)
  • Photos or files you upload for use in your design

When you pay for an order, payment is processed directly by Stripe — we never see or store your full card number. Stripe's own privacy policy governs how they handle payment data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and deliver the design(s) you ordered
  • To communicate with you about your order
  • To process payment through Stripe

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use a couple of trusted services to process some of your information on our behalf:

  • Tally — powers our order form and stores your submitted brief
  • Stripe — processes payments securely

Cookies

This site itself doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. The order form and checkout are embedded from Tally and Stripe, which may set their own cookies to make those embedded tools function correctly — this is governed by their respective privacy policies, not ours.

Your Photos & Uploaded Files

Photos and reference files you upload are used solely to create your ordered design. We may keep a copy on file for your records or future reorders unless you ask us to delete it.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you at any time by emailing us below.
